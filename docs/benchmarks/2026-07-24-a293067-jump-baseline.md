# Baseline — does upstream rtx `a293067` execute the jumps qw-fasttrack built?

**Date:** 2026-07-24
**Build under test:** qw-ctf/rtx `main` @ **`a293067ea89a718bab37d238bde4972ed6c782b0`** (upstream — the build synced this day and running in the LANISTER 2on2).
**Question:** *Does nano's current upstream `main` actually perform the DM3 movement routes qw-fasttrack curated?* This is the **first** such baseline; re-run it on every future upstream sync to track how much of the route work has landed upstream.

## TL;DR

Upstream `a293067` **does not reliably execute qw-fasttrack's routes**. The curated links plant fine into the graph (all 15 `SpeedJump` → `PlanLink`, `adds_ok=15`), but the a293067 executor lands them inconsistently, and the RA climb + rocket-jump mechanics the lab fork handles are **not present upstream yet**.

| RA-tunnel → RA-top (climb up) | **0 / 10** | fails — stalls ~108u short of the armor ledge |
| lifts → mega (under lifts) | **1 / 10** | fails — reaches mega height, rarely lands in box |
| lifts → SNG | **3 / 10** | mostly fails |

**Headline:** of the five jump families, upstream a293067 does **zero** cleanly, one (hexagon north W→E) at coin-flip odds, and cannot even attempt the rocket jump. This is the expected shape — the route curation, the RA-climb executor work, and the RJ planting all live in the lab fork and are not merged upstream.

## Method

- **Instrument:** qw-fasttrack's own harness, driven through the newly-added **framed-msgpack** control client (`fasttrack/mpwire.py` + `core.py`; a293067 moved the game control port from line-text to msgpack — see the port notes below). Auto-detected wire.
- **Trial type:** **goto-proxy** — for each attempt: `Teleport` the bot to the route START, `Goto` the TARGET, observe the outcome. The engine's `Arrived` gate is 24u XY / 48u Z. In addition to the engine verdict, the harness records **raw physics** (peak z, closest XY / 3D approach, full event trajectory) so a physical short-stop is distinguishable from a watchdog false-stall.
  - **Why goto-proxy and not the usual gates:** a293067 has **no** `ra_trial` / `sng_mega` item-goal verbs (those are lab-fork-only). So RA and mega are measured by "put the bot below/at the start, tell it to go to the item, did it make the jump and arrive" — exactly the owner's question ("*from below, up — does it make the ledge jump?*"), just without authentic-pickup semantics. Flagged per route.
- **N = 10** attempts per route, on a **freshly restarted** rig (state-memory hygiene; a293067 additionally has no `penalties` memory).
- **Routes measured:** the RA climb, SNG→mega (+ lifts→SNG), the four individual hexagon links, and bridge/window→RJ.

## Caveats & interpretation

- **goto-proxy, not item-gates.** a293067 has no `ra_trial`/movement-item trial, so RA and mega are goto-proxied. Item-pickup semantics (the lab's authentic RA/mega gates) are not reproduced here.
- **No false-stall contamination.** The engine `Arrived`-count matched the raw box-made count on **every** route — no divergence at the target. a293067's `climbed`-progress rule appears to mitigate the old RA under-target false-stall; the failures here are the bot **physically stopping short** (confirmed by `peak_z` / `min_xy`), not the watchdog mis-firing under the goal.
- **Rocket jump is structurally untestable on a293067.** No RJ links in the graph, RJ disabled in the baseline cfg, and upstream cannot plant one. (A 3-attempt highbridge→RL *goto* probe reached the box 3/3 at peak z20 — but that is **ground pathing, not an RJ** — disregarded for RJ capability.)
- **Baseline cvars matter.** `rtx_walljump`, `rtx_doublejump`, `rtx_bot_rocketjump`, `rtx_bot_ledgecap` were all **0** in this rig config; the numbers are the baseline *as configured above* and are only comparable to re-runs with the same cvars.
- **This is upstream, by design.** The point of the baseline is to measure nano's `main`, not the lab fork (which — being where these routes were built — trivially passes them). Low numbers here are the signal, not a defect: they quantify the gap between upstream and the lab's route work.

## How to reproduce / re-run on the next upstream sync

1. Build the target upstream: `cd rtx-newest && git checkout <sha> && CARGO_TARGET_DIR=$HOME/.cache/rtx-newest-target cargo build --release -p rtx-game`.
2. Fresh rig: `core.server_up(map='dm3', bots=1, lib='~/.cache/rtx-newest-target/release/librtx.so')`; `wait_ready`. (The msgpack client auto-detects the wire; force with `FASTTRACK_MSGPACK=1`.)
3. `core.patch_apply(<scripts/data/dm3-four-route-adds.json>, store=True)`; record `adds_ok`, graph link range, cvar readback, `Items` positions.
4. Run the goto-proxy trials (Teleport→Goto, N=10) for the routes in the table; record raw physics alongside the engine gate.
5. Diff `Made/attempts` per route against this baseline to see what a newer upstream has gained.

**msgpack port note:** measuring any a293067+ build requires the framed-msgpack control client added in `fasttrack/mpwire.py` + `core.py` (verbs with no a293067 equivalent — `ra_trial`, `sjtrace`, `planrjraw`, `plancell`, `unlink` — raise `NotSupported`). Older text-protocol builds still work (auto-detected / `FASTTRACK_MSGPACK=0`).
